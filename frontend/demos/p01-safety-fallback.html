Industrial Safety VR Trainer

Sample onboarding scenario

In a simulated industrial walkthrough, a spill blocks a marked pedestrian route. The learner practices spotting the hazard and choosing an appropriate first action.

The expected response is to stop clear of the spill and alert the site lead so the route can be secured. Walking around the spill leaves the hazard unreported.

This written walkthrough is fallback evidence for the sample. It requires no interaction, headset or external service.
